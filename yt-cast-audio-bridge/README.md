# YT Cast Audio Bridge

Cast YouTube and YouTube Music from your phone to speakers that only support Cast **audio**:
Nest Mini, Google Home, soundbars, AV receivers and speaker groups.

Those speakers do not show up in the YouTube app because YouTube only offers devices with a
screen. This add-on announces one virtual "TV" per speaker. When you cast to it, the add-on
plays the audio of the video on the real speaker and keeps the YouTube app in sync: queue,
next/previous, seek, pause and volume all work.
